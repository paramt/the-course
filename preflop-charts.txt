=== THE COURSE — PREFLOP RANGES (Miller, $1/$2 live) ===

--- OPENING (no raise in front) — RAISE, never limp ---

EP (UTG to 2 off button), 14%:
22+,A2s+,KTs+,QTs+,JTs-76s,AKo,AQo

CO, 22%:
22+,A2s+,K7s+,Q9s+,JTs-43s,J9s-53s,ATo+,KJo+

BTN, 33%:
22+,A2s+,K2s+,Q5s+,J7s+,T9s-43s,T8s-53s,T7s-96s,A7o+,K9o+,QTo+,JTo

BLINDS vs limpers — raise:
99+,ATs+,KJs+,AQo+
(add A5s-A2s,K8s,76s if there's a real chance everyone folds)

SB complete-or-fold in limped pot:
Fold unconnected offsuit with no card above a jack (J4o, 96o, 52o, T3o).
Complete everything else.


--- vs TIGHT RAISE (he limps weak hands) — same from EP, CO, BTN, blinds ---

3-bet:
KK+,A5s

Call:
QQ-22,ATs+,KTs+,QTs+,JTs-76s,AKo

--- vs STEAL RAISE (blinds only; folded to a late-position opener) — 36% defense ---

Call:
88-55,KTs-K8s,K4s-K2s,QJs-QTs,Q8s-Q5s,JTs-T9s,43s,75s-53s,J8s-96s,J7s,ATo-A8o,KJo-K9o,Q9o+,JTo-98o,J9o


--- NOTES ---
Raise-size threshold: small = under 3bb ($6 at 1/2), big = over. Nearly all live
raises are big, so default to the big-raise guidelines from the blinds.
vs a tight raise, position buys you nothing — same range from every seat.
Not in this excerpt: iso-raising limpers from EP/CO/BTN. Default = your full
positional opening range, sized 5-6bb + 1bb per limper.
Do not use the steal-defense block until you confirm the button actually opens
40%+ into folded blinds.
